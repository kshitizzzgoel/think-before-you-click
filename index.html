Think Before
You Click

A hands-on cybersecurity awareness course for college students aged 18–25: 3 animated videos, the production storyboard, interactive tools, a scam simulator and a final exam with certificate. Allow about 50 minutes.

Created by Kshitiz Goel · MCA 3C · VIPS (GGSIPU) · Enrollment No. 17817704425

Lesson 3 · Storyboard

Style: realistic Indian campus, one young Indian student in a navy hoodie with a backpack, navy and teal palette with amber warning accents, clean UI overlays, calm serious tone, no frightening imagery.

Lesson 4 · Scams you will actually meet

About 6 minutes. Open each card and read the “Do” line.

Lesson 5 · Link Inspector (hands-on)

About 5 minutes. Paste any link or try the samples. The check runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. It spots common red flags only, so it can’t prove a link is safe.

Lesson 6 · Password Lab

About 5 minutes. Type a password you do NOT use anywhere, see how long a guessing attack would take, then generate a strong passphrase.

Start typing to see the estimate.

Estimate assumes 20 billion guesses per second against a leaked password database. Longer beats cleverer.

Lesson 7 · UPI and payment safety

About 5 minutes. These are the scams that cost students real money.

Lesson 8 · Spot the Scam (simulator)

About 6 minutes. Eight messages. Decide: scam or safe? Some are genuine.

Lesson 9 · Lock down your accounts

About 6 minutes. Tick what you already do, then do the rest on your phone right now.

Turn on two-factor authentication

Lesson 10 · If you get scammed

About 5 minutes. Speed matters: report as early as you can.

Lesson 11 · Final exam and certificate

About 8 minutes. 10 questions. Score 7 or more to unlock your certificate.
